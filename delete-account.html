Delete your FIGHTR account

App: FIGHTR (com.fightr.app)
Developer: Nishant Agrawal
Contact: fightrclubb@gmail.com
Last updated: 9 October 2026

Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open FIGHTR and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings and scroll to the bottom.
  3. Tap Delete Account Permanently.
  4. Type DELETE and confirm.

Delete without the app

If you cannot sign in or have uninstalled the app, email fightrclubb@gmail.com from the address on your account with the subject "Delete my FIGHTR account". We verify that you own the account and delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your login, profile, plans, workouts, meals, skin and hair routines, water, body measurements, steps, progress photos and profile photos (the files), AI chat sessions and plan jobs, reminders, challenge and rank data, error reports, AI usage counters and any archived chat history.

What is not deleted

  • Copies in hosting logs and database backups until they expire. The backup window is set by our hosting plan and is to be confirmed.
  • Data already processed by DeepSeek, USDA FoodData Central or Open Food Facts, which follows their own retention.
  • Anything you shared yourself through the Android share sheet.

Smaller options

  • Remove a single photo from the Progress screen.
  • Use Reset progress in Settings to clear your progress and keep your account.
  • Uninstalling the app does not delete your cloud data.

Please note

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. FIGHTR has no in-app data export. If you want a copy of your data, email fightrclubb@gmail.com before you delete.
