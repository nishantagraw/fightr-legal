FIGHTR Club rules

App: FIGHTR (com.fightr.app)
Developer: Nishant Agrawal
Safety contact: fightrclubb@gmail.com
Last updated: 25 October 2026

Club is the social part of FIGHTR: posts, Arise (24-hour stories), Aura videos, comments, chats and profiles. Everyone must accept these rules before creating a Club profile. There is zero tolerance for objectionable or abusive content.

The rules

  1. Be 18 or older. Club is for adults. Accounts that look younger are removed.
  2. No nudity or sexual content. Fitness and progress photos are fine. Nudity, sexual content, and anything sexual involving a minor are not.
  3. No bullying, hate or threats. No harassment, body shaming, hate against any group, threats, or encouraging self-harm or eating disorders.
  4. No violence, drugs or dangerous advice. No graphic violence, illegal drugs, selling steroids or other controlled substances, or advice that could seriously hurt someone.
  5. No spam, scams or fake accounts. No selling, fake giveaways, scam links or pretending to be someone else. Only post content you have the right to share.

Report and block

In the app you can report any post, comment, Arise, Aura video, message or profile from its ⋯ menu, and block or mute anyone. Blocked people cannot see your Club profile or contact you. Reported videos and posts are reviewed by us.

How we handle reports

We review reports within 24 hours. Content that breaks these rules is removed, and accounts that break them are warned, suspended or removed. Serious cases (threats to life, child safety) are escalated at once.

Child safety standards

FIGHTR prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM) of any kind, including sexualised images of minors, grooming, sextortion and any content that sexualises children.

  • Club is for adults only (18+), and new profiles start private.
  • Anyone can report content or a profile in the app; child-safety reports are handled first.
  • Violating content is removed and the account is suspended and preserved for law enforcement.
  • We report apparent CSAM to the relevant authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and, in India, through the national cyber crime reporting portal (cybercrime.gov.in), as the law requires.
  • We comply with child safety laws in the countries where FIGHTR is available.

Child safety point of contact: Nishant Agrawal, fightrclubb@gmail.com. If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local police first.
